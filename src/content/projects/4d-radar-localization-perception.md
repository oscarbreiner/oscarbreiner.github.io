Built a real-time 4D radar perception and localization pipeline for high-speed autonomous racing, designed to remain stable under vibration, sensor noise, and Doppler ambiguity at race speeds.

## Highlights

- Developed radar-only odometry with a novel Doppler de-aliasing method for robust ego-motion estimation.
- Validated localization and perception at 250+ km/h in racing conditions.
- Outperformed prior state-of-the-art baselines.
- Contributed to a World Champion finish at A2RL 2025; currently preparing a submission to an automotive journal.
