Master's thesis research on image-to-3D generation, carried out during a research stay at the University of Oxford. The project is a collaboration between the PIXL Lab of Prof. Ronald Clark (Oxford) and the 3D AI Lab of Prof. Angela Dai (TUM).

## Highlights

- Systematic audit of TRELLIS, a state-of-the-art structured-latent model for image-to-3D asset generation.
- Analysis of where and why generated 3D assets diverge from the input image's geometry and appearance.
- Joint supervision across Oxford (PIXL Lab) and TUM (3D AI Lab).
